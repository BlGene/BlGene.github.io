<h1>Max Argus</h1>
<p><a href="mailto:argus.max@gmail.com">argus.max@gmail.com</a></p>
<p>I work on machine learning and robotics, with a focus on learning manipulation from human demonstrations and scaling robot learning and evaluation in simulation. I am currently at the <a href="https://allenai.org/">Allen Institute for AI (Ai2)</a>, where I work on the Molmo robotics projects.</p>
<p>Before that I was a postdoc at the <a href="https://uni-freiburg.de/">University of Freiburg</a>, co-supervised by <a href="https://lmb.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/people/brox/index.en.html">Thomas Brox</a> and <a href="https://rl.uni-freiburg.de/people/valada">Abhinav Valada</a>. My PhD was supervised by Thomas Brox and <a href="https://www.utn.de/person/wolfram-burgard-2/">Wolfram Burgard</a>. I hold a B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Physics from the <a href="https://www.uni-heidelberg.de/en">University of Heidelberg</a> (<a href="https://ommer-lab.com/people/ommer/">Björn Ommer</a>), and interned at <a href="https://bair.berkeley.edu/">BAIR</a> (<a href="http://imaginarynumber.net/">Evan Shelhamer</a>) and Symbio Robotics.</p>
