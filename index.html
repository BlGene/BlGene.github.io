Max Argus

argus.max at gmail.com

I'm currently a Research Scientist at the Allen Institute for AI (Ai2), where I work with Dieter Fox on the robotics team. I am also a postdoc at the University of Washington with Ranjay Krishna.

I work on scaling simulation for robot learning and evaluation. Previously I have worked on learning from demonstrations and diverse machine learning and computer vision problems.

Before that I was a postdoc at the University of Freiburg, working with Abhinav Valada. My PhD was supervised by Thomas Brox and Wolfram Burgard. I hold a B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Physics from the University of Heidelberg (Björn Ommer), and interned at BAIR (Evan Shelhamer) and Symbio Robotics.
