Max Argus

argus.max@gmail.com

I work on machine learning and robotics, with a focus on learning manipulation from human demonstrations and scaling robot learning and evaluation in simulation. I am currently at the Allen Institute for AI (Ai2), where I work on the Molmo robotics projects.

Before that I was a postdoc at the University of Freiburg, co-supervised by Thomas Brox and Abhinav Valada. My PhD was supervised by Thomas Brox and Wolfram Burgard. I hold a B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Physics from the University of Heidelberg (Björn Ommer), and interned at BAIR (Evan Shelhamer) and Symbio Robotics (Jon Long).
